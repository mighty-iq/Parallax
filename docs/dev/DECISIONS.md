- 2026-11-13: The analogical test could not be identified; build a general test-overlap mechanism (About section, per-exercise notes, one-tap check-in, optional helper) and name the test once the owner identifies it (R4).

- 2026-11-13: Known limit kept (B1): a partial Daily attempt resumes by position in the device-filtered list, so a device whose set of runnable exercises changed between the two parts would shift outcomes. The same browser almost never changes what it can run within a day; fixing it changes the saved Daily format, so it waits for a real report.
- 2026-11-13: Hole Punch and Fold: "keep the sheets with a hole at the punch, pick the one most symmetric about the item's creases" (89.6 % / 92.5 %, RV-A) is the format's analytic method (unfold backwards: reflect across each crease), which prep courses teach and the step-by-step strategy card describes; for straight half folds no wrong sheet can pass it on a 4 × 4 sheet. It is reported in the tests as information, not bounded as a cheap heuristic; layer tracking (idle folds, partial flaps) is where it fails and where difficulty rises. Rule 5's cheap heuristics stay bounded.
- 2026-11-13: Angles must also balance the vertex-to-chord-midpoint length (43.2 % before) and answer-only heuristics per resolve level (RV-A): fixed by B5 in chooseArms / chooseFoils with per-level test bounds.
- 2026-11-13: Painted Cubes never asks N = 5, in training or in Section practice (item.asks holds N 1–4 within KRANGE, keys balanced).
- 2026-11-13: The Daily Challenge keeps paused exercises (same items for everyone); the "Taking a test soon?" text says so (RV-B).
- 2026-11-13: "Full section" needs every part reached and at least half the items answered (RV-B: it could be earned with zero answers).
- 2026-11-13: Phones held sideways get two-column layouts (stem left, answers right; Track, Span, Orient and Patterns their own) instead of "turn upright" cards; every item fits 844×390 (B2's fit.py). A turn during a stimulus still voids the trial (H3). PLAN M2's acceptance line reworded.

- 2026-11-13: Section practice departs from R3 §9 in two ways (B7): moving between items is limited to the current part (the PAT lets test-takers move within a section; Parallax times parts separately, so a part closes for good), and forms are numbered without limit (form k is the same for everyone with the same exercise versions) rather than A–C.
- 2026-11-13: Tests the player noted ("Taking a test soon?") and the Check's one-tap answer stay in the JSON backup and its research export, not in the per-trial CSV, which keeps one row per trial.

- 2026-11-13 (B3): Homing is version 2 (seeds give different items: speeds, legs), so 1.0 rows do not mix with new ones. Its route and motion model is shared with Reckon through Homing's definition (lib) and its first-person kit (PX.ex.view('homing').kit), not copied.
- 2026-11-13 (B3): Reckon keeps the player's last 40 answers in the store field 'reckon' for the "your habit over the last 20 routes" line (needs 5) and its chart.
- 2026-11-13 (B3): Homing and Reckon captions are subtitles over the view; the void rule is what harms the task: a freeze over 1 s while moving, slow frames over half the moving time, a lost WebGL context, a change of the view's shape, or a pause (not any 400 ms freeze).
- 2026-11-13 (B3): On touch, Confirm and Here act when the finger lifts on them and cancel that tap's click (Chrome dropped clicks of taps that followed a drag on the view when the machine was busy); mouse, pen and keys use the normal click.
- 2026-11-13 (RV-D): Form foils move a mark only to a congruent face (R3 §8.6); face shapes alone picked the key 71 %. Openings: comparing corner counts and proportions with the solid's outlines is the format's analytic method (taught by the strategy card) and is reported, not bounded; corner count alone, the most-featured face and "the two most alike" are cheap cues and are bounded (features placed without regard to the key; the proportion foil stretches a sibling). Bridges of the DAT-style exercises carry their limitation line (rule 2). Validators of Missing View and Form get independent uniqueness checks (rule 4).
- 2026-11-13 (RV-C): Knots answers are all right (8,100 items re-derived independently). Show me must check every replayed move with an invariant, not crossing count alone (21 of 4,800 replays showed a type-changing step); false captions and the untie tip are reworded; the "shortest over-strand" and "nearest the centre" cues are bounded per rung. Accepted: untie generation p95 35 ms (after Next, never during a stimulus); legibility departures from R2 (stroke 4.5 px, tightest bend about 8 px, open gap about 33 px); the Try item need not be the 5_2.
- 2026-11-13 (RV-H): Spatial Check time is not practice: it stays out of the practice total, as it already stays out of the week, the practice days and the answers; Section practice time counts. Time-boxed sessions cap closing-item replacements again (v1.0 had +25 %). Two-tab merges keep both sides' Section runs, Reckon history, test-hygiene log and newest settings. The clock's reading never runs more than 60 s past the last input, however late its timers fire.
- 2026-11-13 (RV-E): Reckon and Homing point and return answers were mostly predictable from the turns alone ("ignore the legs" 83 % at 40° against 22 % chance; a 130° prior 74 %), a flaw inherited from Homing's v1.0 route model. Decision: fix it in the shared route generator (routes where leg lengths matter, home bearings spread) with per-cell bounds for every turn-only strategy, rather than log an exception: training that turns alone can pass would not train distance integration. Above 'steady', walking time must not predict leg length (|r| ≤ 0.5). The strategy card's side error (since v1.0) is fixed. Trial rows gain an optional 13th column value2 (Reckon: distance ratio on return items; value = signed angle error) so pooled data can separate angle and distance errors.
- 2026-11-13 (B6 fix round): Form: every marks level carries an oriented half mark (with marks moved only to congruent faces, one or two shaded faces on these symmetric solids allow at most two different marked solids); base kept at −2.2, steps to be refitted from pooled exports; the three-sided pyramid is left out; one foil per different marked solid, the mirror arrangement labelled.
- 2026-11-13 (B6 fix round): Openings: features on all three faces at every level, dealt without regard to the key; "check the most featured face only" (46.6 %) is a search order that still needs the outline matched among five openings, bounded at 50 % (its floor is 46.7 % while the key's outline must carry a feature); the box is 5–8 steps so openings and solid share one scale with at least 11 px a step on a 360 px phone; the proportion foil resizes a sibling in 3 of 4 items. Missing View's validator runs a second, independent search on its own renderer.
- 2026-11-13 (G1): Knots carries an "Also in tests" note: "knot or not" items appear in a design entrance exam, a maths competition and a research test of knot reasoning (R2 §1.7); the helper gains the kind "Whether a rope forms a knot when pulled".
