- 2026-11-13: DAT PAT formats ship as four new exercises (Angles, Painted Cubes, Openings, Form), two variants (Fold 4x4, Views "complete the views") and a Section mode; "DAT" appears only descriptively with the ADA line; no score prediction (R3).

- 2026-11-13: The analogical test could not be identified; build a general test-overlap mechanism (About section, per-exercise notes, one-tap check-in, optional helper) and name the test once the owner identifies it (R4).
- 2026-11-13: Phones: every scored item must fit 360x640 without scrolling; a test enforces it (I2).
- 2026-11-13: Defaults where the brief is silent: names Reckon, Knots, Angles, Painted Cubes, Openings, Form; Section mode full (90 items, 60 min) and mini (30 items, 20 min); Sounds setting becomes short synthesized tones, off by default; version 1.1.0 with save migration; separate phone and desktop ratings where screen size matters (Track, Reckon, Angles).

- 2026-11-13: First playable build goes to the owner as a separate private artifact ("Parallax v1.1 preview"), not over the v1.0 artifact, which may already be shared.
- 2026-11-13: Known limit kept (B1): a partial Daily attempt resumes by position in the device-filtered list, so a device whose set of runnable exercises changed between the two parts would shift outcomes. The same browser almost never changes what it can run within a day; fixing it changes the saved Daily format, so it waits for a real report.
- 2026-11-13: Hole Punch and Fold: "keep the sheets with a hole at the punch, pick the one most symmetric about the item's creases" (89.6 % / 92.5 %, RV-A) is the format's analytic method (unfold backwards: reflect across each crease), which prep courses teach and the step-by-step strategy card describes; for straight half folds no wrong sheet can pass it on a 4 × 4 sheet. It is reported in the tests as information, not bounded as a cheap heuristic; layer tracking (idle folds, partial flaps) is where it fails and where difficulty rises. Rule 5's cheap heuristics stay bounded.
- 2026-11-13: Angles must also balance the vertex-to-chord-midpoint length (43.2 % before) and answer-only heuristics per resolve level (RV-A): fixed by B5 in chooseArms / chooseFoils with per-level test bounds.
- 2026-11-13: Painted Cubes never asks N = 5, in training or in Section practice (item.asks holds N 1–4 within KRANGE, keys balanced).
- 2026-11-13: The Daily Challenge keeps paused exercises (same items for everyone); the "Taking a test soon?" text says so (RV-B).
- 2026-11-13: "Full section" needs every part reached and at least half the items answered (RV-B: it could be earned with zero answers).
- 2026-11-13: Phones held sideways get two-column layouts (stem left, answers right; Track, Span, Orient and Patterns their own) instead of "turn upright" cards; every item fits 844×390 (B2's fit.py). A turn during a stimulus still voids the trial (H3). PLAN M2's acceptance line reworded.
- 2026-11-13: A show-me after an answer scrolls the stage above the pinned caption on phones, as Watch's demo does (B2's gap: Orient's dial at 360×640).
- 2026-11-13: Section practice departs from R3 §9 in two ways (B7): moving between items is limited to the current part (the PAT lets test-takers move within a section; Parallax times parts separately, so a part closes for good), and forms are numbered without limit (form k is the same for everyone with the same exercise versions) rather than A–C.
- 2026-11-13: Tests the player noted ("Taking a test soon?") and the Check's one-tap answer stay in the JSON backup and its research export, not in the per-trial CSV, which keeps one row per trial.

- 2026-11-13 (B3): Homing is version 2 (seeds give different items: speeds, legs), so 1.0 rows do not mix with new ones. Its route and motion model is shared with Reckon through Homing's definition (lib) and its first-person kit (PX.ex.view('homing').kit), not copied.

- 2026-11-13 (B3): Homing and Reckon captions are subtitles over the view; the void rule is what harms the task: a freeze over 1 s while moving, slow frames over half the moving time, a lost WebGL context, a change of the view's shape, or a pause (not any 400 ms freeze).
- 2026-11-13 (B3): On touch, Confirm and Here act when the finger lifts on them and cancel that tap's click (Chrome dropped clicks of taps that followed a drag on the view when the machine was busy); mouse, pen and keys use the normal click.

Bridges of the DAT-style exercises carry their limitation line (rule 2). Validators of Missing View and Form get independent uniqueness checks (rule 4).
