**Parallax 1.1: dead reckoning, knots, DAT-style practice, and a proper phone app** 🧊

Free visuospatial trainer, built by the Chimera community: https://mighty-iq.github.io/Parallax/

What's new:
• **Reckon** (experimental): path integration with nothing to steer by. You watch a walk across an empty plain, then point or walk back to the start. No landmarks, no map, and walking speed changes so time won't tell you distance. After each answer you see your angle and distance error and your own bias over recent routes. Thanks to the feedback in this thread: Homing's landmarks were helping too much, so Homing is now the easier stage and Reckon is the real thing.
• **Knots**: does a knot remain when you pull the ends? Which crossing, switched, unties it? Are two loops linked? Every item is proved by knot invariants, and "show me" replays the moves.
• **Six DAT-style formats**: Openings, Missing View, Angles, Hole Punch, Painted Cubes and Form, plus a timed **Section practice** (mini 30 items in 20 minutes, or full 90 in 60). Opt-in through the DAT-style focus, so nobody else's training changes.
• **One honest clock**: practice time now counts only while you're actually practising. Pauses, hidden tabs and idle time no longer eat your session.
• **Phones**: every item now fits a small phone screen without scrolling, Homing works by touch, and phones held sideways get their own layout.
• **Test hygiene**: exercises that share formats with published tests say so, and a "Taking a test soon?" helper can pause them until your test date.

Your v1.0 progress carries over automatically.

Every item is still generated fresh and solver-checked. Parallax doesn't claim to raise IQ or test scores; the Spatial Check measures transfer on tasks you never train.
