**Parallax 1.2: Central Point, a real game layer, rebuilt DAT-style formats** 🧊

Free visuospatial trainer, built by the Chimera community: https://mighty-iq.github.io/Parallax/

What's new:
• **Central Point** (new): tap the spot closest to all the targets. Quick by default: a gut estimate against a tightening deadline; near misses still score. The exact form is a variant.
• **DAT-style formats rebuilt**: Angles, Painted Cubes, Openings, Hole Punch, Form and Missing View follow the real formats more closely (lettered options, plain drawings, figure sets with a tally pad) and start gentler. Still opt-in.
• **Game layer**: the Climb, grades, an Atlas of what you've handled, challenge codes, result cards, and a Daily Challenge everyone plays identically. No streaks: missing a day costs nothing.
• **Faster progress**: each exercise tracks every dimension of its difficulty, spots a plateau, changes something and tells you why.
• **First session**: one two-minute tutorial, a starting-point check (about 11.5 minutes), then training.
• **Spatial Check, redesigned**: new near, mid and far tasks you never train, plus a verbal control, so change is judged against familiarity, not zero.
• **Transfer bets**: every exercise names the mechanism it bets on, and new add-ons (compare, bridge, draw the answer, calibrate) test those bets. Calibration's transfer is untested, so you're helping test it.
• **Offline**: once opened, it starts without a connection; install it on your phone.

Your 1.1 progress carries over automatically.

Every item is generated fresh and solver-checked. Parallax doesn't claim to raise IQ or test scores; the Spatial Check measures change on tasks you never train.
