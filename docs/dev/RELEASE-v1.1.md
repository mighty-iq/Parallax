# RELEASE v1.1: the owner's five-minute check

What only a person can judge. Use the private preview on your phone (and a computer if handy). Reply "yes" to publish, or tell me what felt wrong.

1. **Open it.** The Chimera pop-up appears; its discord.gg/chmr link opens Discord. Close it; tap Library once: it opens.
2. **Reckon (2 min).** Library → Reckon → Watch, Try, Go. Does the walk look smooth, and is it comfortable to watch? Point home once, walk back once. Is the feedback (angle, distance, your habit) clear?
3. **Homing on the phone.** Turn by dragging the view and with ±1° / ±5°. A swipe that starts on a button should scroll, not turn.
4. **Knots.** Are the over/under gaps easy to see on your screen? Is the Watch clear about "your rope" and "the copy"?
5. **One DAT-style format and a mini Section.** Try Painted Cubes or Angles, then Library → DAT-style → Mini section for a minute; end it and open the review.
6. **DAT-style focus.** Switch it on in the Library: Today's plan should now lead with the formats and say so. Switch it off again.
7. **The clock.** Start Today's session, pause for a minute, come back: the minutes shown should not include the pause.
8. **Sound.** Settings → Sounds on: short tones after answers, never during a moving stimulus. Is the volume all right?
9. **The guide.** Skim "What's new", Reckon, Knots and the DAT-style pages in docs/Parallax-Guide.pdf.
10. **The Discord post.** Read docs/dev/DISCORD-v1.1.md (under 2,000 characters). Change anything you like before posting.

When you say yes: I merge `build` into `main`, GitHub Pages updates in a minute or two, and your v1.0 players' progress carries over on their next visit.
