## Done (measured in node, scratch)
- Math core: slot diagrams (PD convention), faces, R1/R2/R3 moves (R3 by rewiring), greedy + bounded R3
  search with a move certificate, Fox determinant, Kauffman bracket → Jones, linking number, components.
  Checked: 3_1 (w −3: t⁻¹+t⁻³−t⁻⁴), 4_1, 5_1, 5_2 Jones and determinants; Jones invariant under every
  R3 and simplification; faces = n + 2.
- Geometry: uniform cubic B-spline through integer control points (exact Bézier for SVG), samples at
  interval midpoints (no rounding ties at lattice crossings), typed-array crossing grid, legibility
  (angles, 36 px crossings, strand gap per level, no near-triple points, bend radius, ends off the plate).
- Outlines: one lattice walker (momentum, alongside penalty, rewind on stuck) for open ropes and loops.
  Spacing: open pitch 37 (gap ≥ 30), close 28 (≥ 24, crossings never side by side), tight 24 (≥ 20,
  crossings never on any of 8 neighbours). 0 legibility failures in 1 200 unchecked outlines.
- pull: knot/unknot matched pairs on alternations (open and closed), writhe and visible slides.
  Ladder (measured feasibility): 5, 6, 7, 8, 8 slide-over, 9, 9s, 10s, 11s, 12s crossings.
  180 items valid, 89 knots; hardest rung p95 ≈ 18 ms.
- untie / tie: ≥ 7 crossings; one (slide-off, twist) group; alternation-change and handedness patterns
  symmetric; key's half and distance rank drawn from the seed. Shortcut rates 26–36 % (chance 25 %).
- link: ring loop A + weaving loop B, 6 crossings between them, 8–11 in all; pairs matched on the A-over-B
  count (always even), alternations, writhe, slides; unlinked only by a split certificate. 120 valid.
