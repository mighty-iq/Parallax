## Fix round 2 (guide reviewer: pull seed 530622980 at PX_TEST_ITEMS=2000)
- Cause: not an illegal move and not a gap in the generator's checks. In step 4 (a loop dropped under),
  at k = 0.5137 the moving run is a straight diagonal line; two of its non-adjacent pieces are parallel to
  rounding error (cross product 5.6e-17), and the frame crossing finder (polyHit) divided by that and
  reported a phantom crossing at a segment end. The phantom made the frame's determinant 7, which the test
  read as a strand passing through another. The window is < 0.0002 wide in k (0.51365–0.5138), so a
  199-point scan of the same move found nothing and the default sample never hit it.
- Fix: polyHit and the ramp check's segCross treat segments whose |sin| < 1e-9 as parallel (no
  crossing). This also removes the phantom from the drawing (a stray casing for one frame).
