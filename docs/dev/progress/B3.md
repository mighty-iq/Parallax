### B1 (turn-only shortcuts): what the route levels allow, with numbers
- Measured now (tests/node/*: shortcutReport, the reviewer's definitions, CV constants, n ≈ 420 per cell):
  Reckon point: no cell within chance + 10 (equal legs over in 22 of 24 cells, the 130° prior 21, reverse the
  last turn 11, always the same angle 12, mirror 0). Reckon return: 7 of 24 cells within (2/2, 3/1, 3/2, 5/1,
  5/2, 6/2, 7/2); last leg only and average distance (turn around) over in 0. Homing: no cell within (equal
  legs 20, prior 23, reverse last turn 11, fixed 11, mirror 0).
- Proof that the generator cannot fix routes 0, 1, 2, 4 (2 legs, or small turns): in every valid route of these
  levels the start lies on the side of the net turn (pools of 22,000–113,000 routes: 0 % opposite), so a fixed
  turn toward that side hits at least 2·tol/180 = 44 / 33 / 22 % against bounds of 32 / 27 / 21 %.
- At 40° every route is out: the axis gap (21°, set by the mirror rule (tol + 2)/2) leaves 276° of answers, so a
  fixed angle alone gets ≥ 29 % of uniformly spread keys; with the other strategies the best is about 33 %.
- Best any route distribution can do (minimax over a pool, multiplicative weights; scratch b3/lp.mjs): against
  the listed strategies only 7 of 24 point cells are feasible even in principle (routes 3, 5, 7 at 30° and
  20°, route 6 at 30°), and only with a handful of hand-picked routes; against the wider turn-only family
  (equal legs ± an offset, 180 − last turn ± an offset) 3 cells. Dropping the heading-not-updated rule does
  not help. No diverse design found meets the bound in any cell: target-first, side-first, contrast legs and
  forced misses each trade one shortcut for another (forced equal-legs misses create "equal legs + 50°": 46–58 %).
- So the generator is unchanged (any change would trade shortcuts and churn validated items).
  Proposal for the lead (any one):
  (a) scoped exception, logged: turn-only strategies are partial path integration (they integrate every turn);
      rule 5 for Reckon/Homing bounds only the no-integration ones (fixed angle, turn around, mirror, reversed,
      heading not updated) where geometry allows; the Library says "on the first levels the turns alone tell
      you most of the answer"; the continuous errors (value, value2) keep the leg part measurable;
  (b) restructure (a design spike, not a tweak): rated route levels only with 3–4 legs, medium/large turns and
      tolerances ≤ 30°; 2-leg and small-turn levels become unrated warm-ups. Even then no generator is proven;
  (c) scoring change: grade the continuous error, which the engine's binary ratings do not support today.
  B3 recommends (a) now and (b) as a v1.2 study.
- Return items: "true direction + average distance" hits 52–97 % in every cell because the disk allows ±50 / 35 /
  25 % of the home distance and home distances span only 5–20 m: tightening the return tolerances, not the
  generator, would change that. It needs the direction solved, so it is reported, not bounded.
