  (R3 §8.6, lead's must-fix), one or two shaded faces on these symmetric solids allow at most two different marked
  solids (a box with one or two shaded faces only one), so three valid mark foils need an oriented mark. Base kept at
  −2.2 by the 90 %-at-θ=0 rule; the step values are to be refitted from pooled exports.
- Form: 'tet' left out of the items (no four-sided face for the half mark); rectangular pyramids appear only where
  their base can show the half mark (4 % of items).
- Form: mark foils are one per different marked solid (arrangements a turn carries onto each other count once); the
  mirror arrangement is labelled 'mirror' and drawn on the same solid.
- Openings: features on all three faces at every level (1+1+1, 2+1+1, 2+2+1, 2+2+2), counts dealt at random; the
  "most featured face only" partial strategy is bounded at 50 % (a third of 1 + two thirds of a fifth = 46.7 % is
  the floor while the key's outline must carry a feature).
- Openings: the box is 5–8 steps (not 6–10) and no option is longer than 8 steps, so the openings can be drawn at
  the solid's scale with at least 11 px a step on a 360 px phone; the view draws the solid no larger than the
  openings (one scale), the texts say so.
- Openings: the proportion foil is a resized sibling in 3 of 4 items and the key resized in 1 of 4 (all sibling
  makes "avoid the most alike pair" an inverse cue); resizing also squeezes, so it stays within 8 steps.
- Openings: keyview levels mix faces ([front, front, side], [front, side, top], [side, top, top]); each face is
  the key in a third of items overall.

  structures. drawRay now uses a typed voxel array.
