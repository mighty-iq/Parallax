# Parallax against the owner's standing rules: what v1.2 must change

Audit of 14 Nov 2026 on branch `build` at 83ab453 (the released v1.1.0 code of 224228f; the later commit changes only STATE.md and DECISIONS.md). Read: js/, css/, index.html, README.md, LICENSE files, docs/guide/guide.html, docs/dev/*.md (ENGINE, DESIGN-PLAN, ARCHITECTURE, PLAN, STATE, DECISIONS, VERIFY-v1.1, reviews, progress), docs/research/, tests/e2e/ (grep only), .gitignore. Nothing was run. Standing rules: the owner's project rules of 11 Nov 2026 (kept outside this repository and called RULES.md below; Parallax switches to them in v1.2 by decision D1). Parallax's own rules: CLAUDE.md lines 17–29 (rules 1–12).

After this audit the standing rules gained a section on interactive check-ins: a decision board with the plan message, a playtest mode in test builds, and the release checklist as a page. PLAN-v1.2.md covers them.

Ratings: **MEETS** · **PARTLY** · **MISSING** · **CONFLICTS** (a Parallax rule forbids it; the rule is named). Sizes: S under an hour, M a few hours, L a day or more. Line numbers refer to 83ab453.

## Summary

67 requirements rated: **MEETS 35 · PARTLY 21 · MISSING 8 · CONFLICTS 3.**

Biggest gaps, by weight:
1. **Measurement cannot show one person's gain within weeks** (ME5, MISSING, L). With a real transfer of half the training gain, 96.5 % of simulated learners still read "unclear yet" after 60 days (ENGINE.md:173), because each check is compared with the baselines on its own. There are no near or far probes either (ME1).
2. **No plateau-breaking** (PB1–PB3, MISSING, L). Nothing detects a stall across sessions. The engine's adaptations (interleaving stage back, weak spots, the bandit's shares) run silently.
3. **The game layer is quiet by rule** (F6, CONFLICTS rule 11, L). Rule 11 forbids points and leaderboards outright. RULES.md wants more game, built in versions that keep the pull without the harm.
4. **Transfer bets are not tagged or instrumented** (T1 PARTLY, T2 MISSING, L). Exercises carry ability codes, not the mechanism each bets on. Relational comparison was planned but not built, bridging is a paragraph of text, and show-me use is not logged.
5. **The voice stacks disclaimers by rule** (S5, CONFLICTS rule 2, L). About, the README and the guide lead with what training cannot do. Rule 2 puts a limitation line beside every "where this is used" text.
6. **Chimera details** (C2 CONFLICTS rule 1; C4, C6, C8–C10, C13; M in total). The full pop-up opens on every launch. The tutorial end screens have no link, and the README's first line has none. There are no invitations at personal bests, milestones, plateaus or exports, and a few lines say more about Chimera than the fixed text.
7. **Fast progress** (P1, P2, PARTLY, L and M). There is one rating per exercise across all dimensions, Train is untimed, and early gains are worded as noise.

Already strong: the item engine and verification, alternate forms, a poolable export, the Daily Challenge with cards and codes, the permanent link, the guide's links, licences, data handling, the state files and BRIEF.md hygiene.

## 7. Open questions for the owner

1. **How much game, and in what form?** Recommended: points for skill only (difficulty-weighted right answers) with ranks on top of the honest levels, ~~a gain-framed daily chain with automatic freezes~~ (withdrawn by the owner, 14 Nov: no Duolingo-style streaks), and a seeded weekly community challenge with a result card. Comparison stays in the Discord, through cards and codes. There would be no in-app leaderboard: none is possible without a server, and research links leaderboards to lower motivation in low scorers. Alternative: keep levels as the only number and add celebrations, quests and the weekly challenge. This trades the Fun goal against the research cautions in D-motivation.md.
2. **How much time may measurement cost, so that a real gain shows within weeks?** Recommended: a trend model over all checks plus short embedded probe blocks (about 3 minutes, untrained and without feedback, in every third session), with the full check kept monthly. Alternatives: longer standalone checks (25–35 minutes every two weeks), or the current 15–25 minute check with "unclear yet" for months. This trades training time against the Measurement goal.
3. **Baseline first, or play first?** Recommended: a 5–6 minute baseline inside the first session, right after a two-minute tutorial, and the second baseline before the next session's rated items. Alternatives: the full baseline before any rated training (cleaner, but a slower start), or optional as now (a fast start, but a weaker familiarity estimate). This trades the two-minute start against clean baselines.

Not forks, but RULES.md asks for the owner's yes: putting the README and docs prose under CC BY-SA 4.0 (a licence change, L1), and the lines that say more about Chimera than the fixed text (C13).
