## 0. Summary: the most important findings, each with its fix

1. **The recommended measurement options cannot meet the plan's own week-8 acceptance, and the board promises it.** M1 §4.1 and §5.4 give Design A (the 12-minute default, with 10-minute baselines) a detectable composite gain at week 8 of 0.85 SD (central) or 0.57 SD (optimistic). The only configuration M1 reports at or below 0.5 SD is 4.5-minute blocks plus 16-item (18-minute) baselines under the optimistic set: 0.49, analytic only. Yet L18 says a 0.5 SD gain shows "around week 8", L88 makes "≤ 0.5 SD at week 8" a pass/fail acceptance, and the board's 12-minute option says the same. M1 §8.5 keeps the week-8 figure as reported until the owner restates the target, and DECISIONS (18 Nov) says the target goes to the owner as a "time-and-target decision". Decision 2 asks only for time. **Fix:** add the target to decision 2 (M1 §5.5 options 1–5), report the week-8 figure in L88 instead of gating on it, and correct L18 and the board (A1, C1).
2. **Milestone 6 leaves out the probe upgrades that every "optimistic" number assumes** (M1 §4.3: Cube Match with 4 options, Mechanisms with 4 options, Pattern Memory scored by cells, items under 10 s). **Fix:** add them to milestone 6, each a new probe version that is never mixed into one trend with 1.1 data (M1 §9 #7). Otherwise drop the optimistic claims from L18, L30 and L88 (C2).
3. **The coverage claim is not true.** L3 says every O-item and all 32 gaps are placed. O34 is placed nowhere. O13, O23, O35, O36 and O49 are only implied. O50 and O51 are only partly placed. Fourteen gap IDs are implied without being named. STATE.md's "a script checked this" most likely counted the "O1–O61" range in L3 and L7. **Fix:** name every ID where its work sits, and close O34 explicitly (§B).
4. **The first-band acceptance (L56) fails on D2's own numbers once σ0 = 1.5 is adopted.** Openings is predicted at 75 %, below the 76 % floor (D2 §3.8). With σ0 = 1.0, four exercises reach the old floor only after item 10. The test D2 §3.9 specifies uses 74–84 % and ≥ 66 %. **Fix:** adopt §3.9's thresholds and run both criteria with σ0 1.5 (A14).
5. **The plateau acceptance contradicts itself.** L70 allows up to 5 % false alarms among improving learners, then requires "no learner flagged while still rising". **Fix:** replace that phrase with P1 §2.5–2.6's checks, and add the 0.20-slope and 600-answer criteria and the non-responder check (A10, C4).
6. **CLAUDE.md numbering collides.** L59 says "13–15 new", but CLAUDE.md rule 13 is already Privacy, which overrides every other rule. **Fix:** keep Privacy as rule 13 and number the new rules 14–16 (C6).
7. **The legal statement overstates D1.** L22 says no live patent "exists". D1 §4.1/§4.5 says none was found, in a search that was not a freedom-to-operate search. L22 also drops "exercise" names, logo or styling, implied approval and prep products' explanations from the off-limits list. **Fix:** use the corrected wording in A16.
8. **The Central Point default departs from T1 without saying so.** T1 §5.4 reserves any marker of the average position for every format-declaring artefact, tutorial demos included. Separately, C1 §5.4's Try 2 shows the average in its reveal. **Fix:** record the tutorial Watch as a deliberate, test-exempted exception, and drop the average from Try 2 (A24, C7).
9. **The Reckon gentle-mode default does not hold arithmetically.** By N1 §5.3's own formula, a 3-leg route with large turns takes about 32 s at gentle values, and about 35 s with the leg levels' final rotation. The generator then speeds plans up to fit 30 s, which breaks the gentle speeds. **Fix:** cap turns or path length too and test that the cap holds, or accept up to 40 s (A17).
10. **Several decisions the notes leave to the lead are neither defaults nor owner questions.** All are rule-5 exceptions or consent questions: C1's "nudge the average" (33–76 %), N1's leg levels (turn-only answers up to 50 %), D2's Form and Hole Punch band methods, D1's format methods, T1's "Join the bet trials" switch and its power simulation. **Fix:** add them to Defaults with DECISIONS lines (C17).
11. **Board wording.** "Proof that it works for me" and "Real DAT/PAT practice" (ranking), "These pay for time" (option C), "Measures far transfer" and the concept lines promise or overstate. The intro omits Homing, which changes. The Form card says "Plain shapes first" against D2's shaded-face band. **Fix:** see §D and C8–C9.
12. **Privacy:** the old draft's line that `main` receives the 17 Nov privacy cleanup with v1.2 has disappeared. **Fix:** restore it in milestone 2 or 11 (B.3).
13. **Smaller items.** Milestones 8–10 have no acceptance, while gap R2 asks for one per milestone. D2's re-basing is described as a uniform rise (A13). N1 citations use ambiguous "Q" numbers. The board lists 8 of ACCESS.md's top 10 sources.

## Method notes and limits

- The O-id and gap-ID lists came from a regular-expression pass over the plan, PLAN-v1.3 and the old draft. Each hit was read in context, because note names (P1, T1, M1, C1, D1, D2) match gap IDs.
- A17 is the reviewer's arithmetic, using the formula and constants named there; it does not run the generator.
- The board was reviewed from its data file and, for the Central Point sketch, the rule block of its page. The sketches were not played.
- The notes' own facts (for example the studies they summarise) were not re-checked against their sources; this review checks the plan against the notes.
